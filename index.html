About Me

Welcome to my Webpage. I am Behzad Joudat, a third-year PhD candidate in Computer Science with an emphasis on Hardware Security at Thomas J. Watson School of Engineering and Applied Science, Binghamton University.
I have over five years of experience in computer hardware, embedded systems, and FPGA design. Skilled in FPGA development, system verification, and machine learning acceleration using C, Python, VHDL, Verilog, Vivado, and Quartus. Adept at bridging software and hardware for optimized, high-performance, and energy-efficient solutions.

My research interests include:

  • Computer Architecture
  • Hardware Security
  • Microarchitectural Side-Channels
  • ASIC & FPGA design
  • Embedded Systems

Research

My recent research focuses on FPGA-based architectures, with an emphasis on power monitoring and side-channel attack analysis. I explore how hardware-level implementations can be optimized for performance and energy efficiency while maintaining robust security. By leveraging real-time power measurements and FPGA prototyping, my work aims to uncover and mitigate vulnerabilities arising from power side channels, contributing to the development of more secure and trustworthy hardware systems.
